swarm-build GitHub

Ask your agent. A swarm builds your screenshot.

swarm-build is a tiny JS module and an agent skill. Your coding agent looks at the image, picks the regions a designer would draw, and a swarm of cursors builds it in front of your visitors.

    1. Add the skill

    Claude Code: copy skills/swarm-build/ into ~/.claude/skills/. Other agents: point them at SKILL.md.

    2. Ask for it

    "Use /swarm-build on the hero screenshot." The agent copies three files and chooses the regions by looking at your image.

    3. Ship it

    One dependency-free module. Respects reduced motion, works under a strict CSP, and removes itself when done.
